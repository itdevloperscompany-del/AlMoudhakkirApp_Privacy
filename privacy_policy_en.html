Privacy Policy – Al_moudhakkir_app

Last updated: October 20, 2026

The Al_moudhakkir application is developed by the development team to support you in learning, memorizing, and reviewing the Holy Quran. Protecting your privacy and personal data is an absolute priority.

1. Information We Collect

a. Authentication data (Google Sign-In)

When you choose to sign in with your Google account, we only collect the basic information needed to manage your profile:

  • Your display name
  • Your email address

b. Learning and progress data

To provide personalized tracking of your memorization (Hifz) and multi-device synchronization, we store:

  • The surahs and verses you have memorized or are currently learning
  • Your revision statistics
  • Your favorites (settings)

c. Technical and usage data

Non-identifying technical data (device model, operating system version, crash reports) may be collected automatically to improve the stability of the application.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to:

  • Allow you to create an account and sign in securely.
  • Save and synchronize your memorization progress.
  • Personalize your learning experience.
  • Improve performance and fix potential bugs.

We do not sell, rent, or share any of your personal data with third parties for advertising or commercial purposes.

3. Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Google's infrastructure services (Firebase / Google Cloud), which meet the highest encryption standards (in transit and at rest).

4. Your Rights and Account Deletion

In accordance with data protection regulations and Google Play rules:

  • You can view, modify, or reset your memorization data at any time.
  • You have the right to request the complete deletion of your account and all associated data.
  • To request the deletion of your data, you can do so directly from the application settings or by writing to us by email.
